# Storypress

**Every life has a front page.**

Storypress helps people turn their memories into illustrated personal newspapers worth keeping. Speak or type, answer a few thoughtful questions, and shape the result in your own words. No writing experience or technical confidence needed.

Built at the AI Engineer MLH hackathon, Storypress is designed with older storytellers, families, and multilingual households in mind.

## Start with your story

- **A life memory** — preserve an everyday moment, a family tradition, or a chapter of your past.
- **A love story** — remember how a relationship began and the moments that made it yours.
- **A new chapter** — reflect on a move, graduation, career change, or another beginning.

Each starting point guides the opening question and interview focus. The conversation follows what you share; you can finish whenever you are ready.

## From memory to keepsake

- **Tell it your way.** Type, record an answer to review as text, or choose a spoken interview. English, Chinese, and Spanish are supported.
- **Bring a photograph.** Choose whether AI can help notice details and suggest questions. You can review and correct its clues.
- **Rediscover the setting.** Optional web research can surface relevant places and historical context, with sources kept separate from your personal recollections.
- **Make it yours.** Edit the newspaper, choose included artwork, or request a story-inspired illustration or three-panel comic. AI artwork is labeled as illustration.
- **Keep your progress.** Guest work carries through sign-in in the same browser. Signed-in stories save privately in **My stories**.
- **Take it with you.** Review your edition, download a copy, or print it—including saving as PDF through your browser.

## A work in progress

Storypress is an early prototype in a private pilot. Public sign-up, books, and payments are not available yet. Approved editions can use revocable, unlisted links on deployments where sharing is enabled. Mobile voice support still needs testing across real devices.
